Departure of Directors or Certain Officers ; Election of Directors ; Appointment of Certain Officers ; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. On November 13, 2026, Paul Lohrey was appointed to the Board of Directors of iShares Delaware Trust Sponsor LLC (the “Sponsor”), which is the sponsor of iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (the “Trust”). Mr. Lohrey was appointed by BlackRock Asset Management International Inc., which is the member of the Sponsor. Also on November 13, 2026, Mr. Lohrey was appointed as a member of the Sponsor’s Audit Committee. Mr. Lohrey, 64, served as Interim Chief Executive Officer of the Center for Research in Security Prices, LLC (“CRSP”) during January-February 2026 and as Managing Director, Corporate Strategy and Analytics at CRSP from June 2023 to December 2025. From June 2010 until he retired in June 2022, Mr. Lohrey was a Managing Director at BlackRock, Inc. His roles at BlackRock included Head of iShares and BlackRock U.S. ETF Product Engineering from July 2014 to June 2022 and President and Chief Executive Officer of the Sponsor from November 2015 to June 2022. Mr. Lohrey’s experience prior to joining BlackRock included serving as Chief Investment Officer for Europe for The Vanguard Group from October 2008 to May 2010. Mr. Lohrey earned a Bachelor of Arts in economics from Duke University in 1984 and an MBA in finance from the University of Chicago in 1986. In addition, effective November 13, 2026, Peter Landini resigned as a Director of the Sponsor. Mr. Landini’s decision to resign was not the result of any dispute or disagreement with the Sponsor or the Trust on any matter relating to the Trust's operations, policies or practices.
